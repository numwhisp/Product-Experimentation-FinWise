# FinWise · Hypothesis & Bet, Module 1

## Exercise 1 · First hypothesis
- **Hypothesis:** I think FinWise’s biggest problem is a broken onboarding and activation experience within the reverse-trial flow, causing users to drop off before reaching their "aha moment" and discovering core product value.
- **Evidence:** Evidence

Severe Funnel Leakage (2% Conversion): Converting only 2% of trial users into paying customers indicates a massive gap between initial user intent and value realization during the reverse-trial period.

Low One-Year Retention (40% Retention / 60% Churn): High first-year churn signals that even users who convert were likely insufficiently onboarded into habits that drive long-term retention.

Paid Acquisition Bottleneck: Paid ad spend has hit diminishing returns. Top-of-funnel volume is sufficient, but compounding growth is blocked by monetizing and retaining the traffic already coming through the door.
- **Bet:** My Bet

First Test: A guided, outcome-focused onboarding wizard targeting primary financial setup tasks (bank integration & balance sheet view).

Why:
Fixing activation at the top of the product funnel yields the highest ROI. Improving the trial-to-paid conversion rate doubles the throughput of FinWise's existing paid marketing spend without requiring additional ad budget. Furthermore, users who build setup momentum on Day 1 are far more likely to integrate FinWise into daily financial routines, directly mitigating the 60% annual churn rate.

## Testable hypothesis
- **IF** we introduce an interactive, 3-step setup checklist on first login that guides users to connect a bank account and generate their first automated financial summary within 10 minutes.
- **THEN** THEN Day-7 Core Activation Rate WILL increase BY 25% (from ~15% to ~40%), lifting overall trial-to-paid conversion from 2% to 3.5%.
- **BECAUSE** reverse trials give users full access, but without structured setup guidance, small business owners experience feature overload, fail to see actionable financial insights, and abandon the trial.
- **MEASURED WITH** An A/B test run over 4 weeks targeting all new trial sign-ups, measuring Day-1 Core Action completion as the leading indicator and 14-day Trial Conversion Rate as the primary outcome metric.

The bottleneck is not onboarding usability—it is the reverse-trial feature gating and value paywall structure, which allows users to extract utility for free without encountering a compelling trigger to convert to a paid subscription.
